## Check every project answer

| Check | A strong answer includes |
|---|---|
| Scope | The service, user need, and your responsibility |
| Contribution | What you personally decided or implemented |
| Judgment | An alternative, constraint, or trade-off |
| Delivery | Relevant collaboration, rollout, or recovery details |
| Result | A supported metric or observable improvement |
| Reflection | What you learned or would change |

This is a practice checklist, not Viki's hiring rubric. If you cannot explain how a metric was measured, use the qualitative outcome rather than inventing precision.

## How to handle a question you cannot answer fully

For a missing project detail, say you do not recall the exact figure and explain the evidence you do remember. A precise, bounded answer is more useful than guessing.
