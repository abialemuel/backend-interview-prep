## What the company does

geoSurge describes its business as helping brands understand and improve how generative AI systems represent them. Its thesis includes both information retrieved at answer time and information already learned by models. It calls its approach **Corpus Engineering**. Treat the proposed ability to improve representation as the company's thesis; ask how it demonstrates results. [Company overview](https://web.geosurge.ai/about)

In plain language: when someone asks an AI assistant which product or company to choose, geoSurge wants to help brands understand whether they appear, how they are described, and what could improve that representation.

The company announced a **$12 million seed round in July 2026**, led by **AlbionVC**. Its announcement says funding will support research and engineering hiring, infrastructure, and product development. Funding is useful context; it does not establish current runway or profitability. [Company funding announcement](https://web.geosurge.ai/posts/geosurge-raises-12m-behind-a-new-thesis-for-ai-visibility)

Its careers page emphasizes autonomy, ownership, and an async-friendly, remote-first approach. Eligibility to work from your location and required working hours still need confirmation for this vacancy. [Careers](https://geosurge.ai/careers)
