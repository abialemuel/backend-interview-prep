## Salary, availability, location, and relocation

These facts are not established by your CV. Prepare them before the call:

| Topic | Answer structure |
|---|---|
| Current employment and location | Your actual status and residence; distinguish employer location from where you live |
| Notice period | The contractual notice period and a realistic start window |
| Expected compensation | Ask for the range, currency, pay period, and package structure; give your chosen range if required |
| Current compensation | Separate base, allowances, bonus, and currency accurately |
| Work authorization | Your actual status and whether you need employer support for the hiring country |
| Relocation | Your genuine willingness, constraints, and realistic timing |
| Other interviews | Actual processes and confirmed deadlines; interviews are not offers |

A useful compensation opening:

> Could you share the approved range and package structure for this role? I'd like to understand the location, level, and benefits so we can align expectations on the same basis.

If the role is confirmed in Dubai, ask whether compensation is quoted as monthly AED base or an all-inclusive package. Do not reuse the SGD range from your Viki preparation.

## Questions to ask HR

Pick four or five, depending on what has already been explained:

- Which squad, product domain, and level is this role hiring for?
- What customer or business problem would this person initially own?
- Which languages does the team use, and how much prior .NET/C# experience is needed?
- How does the team practise pairing, mob programming, and TDD day to day?
- How do you assess someone with strong delivery-domain experience who is still developing formal XP practice?
- What are the location, working arrangement, compensation range, and relocation support for this vacancy?
- What follows HR, and will any coding interview involve pairing or TDD?
