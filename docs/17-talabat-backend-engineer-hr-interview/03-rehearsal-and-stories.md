## Build one customer-centered story

Use a real AlArabi workflow and answer:

1. Who had the problem: partner operations, restaurant staff, an internal team, or customers?
2. What was difficult or manual before the integration?
3. How did you learn about that problem?
4. What did you build and what did others own?
5. How did you validate that the workflow improved?

The CV supports significantly reduced manual onboarding effort. It does not establish an exact percentage, direct customer interviews, or an increase in revenue.
